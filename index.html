How this scan works

SCANNING HIERARCHY

Every candidate is walked through the same funnel, in order, before it can appear above:

  1. Market regime
  2. Sector leadership
  3. Relative strength (RS-20/50/100/200)
  4. Stage analysis
  5. Price structure (VCP, pivot, base)
  6. Volume
  7. Momentum
  8. Breakout quality
  9. Risk / reward

SCORING

A 100-point Master Score is only awarded where real data supports it. Categories with no reliable data are marked N/A and dropped from the score rather than defaulted to zero — the total is then rescaled proportionally, and the dropped categories are disclosed. A candidate with too many N/A categories to score meaningfully is left unscored, as shown above, rather than assigned a misleading number.

DAILY REFRESH WORKFLOW (MOBILE-FRIENDLY)

After market close (bhavcopy is usually live ~6:30–7:00 PM IST), open pivot_scanner.ipynb in Colab and run it. It downloads the official NSE bhavcopy, filters to the Nifty 500, pulls price history for shortlisted movers via yfinance, and generates a complete, ready-to-open HTML file — today's scan is baked directly into the page. Download that one file from Colab and open it. No separate JSON file, no server, no CORS issues — works the same on mobile as on desktop.
